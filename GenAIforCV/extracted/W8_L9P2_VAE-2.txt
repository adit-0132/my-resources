Figure Source: Song, Chunlin & Sudirman, Sud & Merabti, Madjid. (2010). A Spatial and Frequency Domain Analysis of the Effect of Removal Attacks on Digital Image Watermarks.
Example: Variational Auto-Encoder
Concept: Fei-Fei Li, Ehsan Adeli, Generative Modelling, Stanford Lecture, 2024
In a Variational Autoencoder (VAE), each latent attribute is represented not as a fixed value but as a probability distribution. 

This probabilistic representation allows the model to sample different values from the latent distributions and pass them through the decoder to generate images that exhibit similar characteristics to those in the training data. 

Unlike a traditional autoencoder, where the primary objective is to reconstruct the input image, a VAE can generate entirely new images that may not have existed in the training dataset. 

Thus, the VAE is not limited to reconstruction; it learns a continuous and structured latent space from which new, plausible samples can be generated, giving it its inherently generative nature.
