Named Entities
• Named entity, in its core usage, means anything that can be
referred to with a proper name. Most common 4 tags:
• PER (Person): “Marie Curie”
• LOC (Location): “New York City”
• ORG (Organization): “Stanford University”
• GPE (Geo-Political Entity): "Boulder, Colorado"
• Often multi-word phrases
• But the term is also extended to things that aren't entities:
• dates, times, prices
Source: https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3 .

forcing to force each input to the
correct gold value for training. We

the

compute the loss at each token,

loss is then propagated through the
decoder parameters and the encoder
parameters.
