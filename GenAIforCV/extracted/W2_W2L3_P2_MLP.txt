Multi-Layer Perceptron
MLP (with one or more hidden layers): The strength of a Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) lies in its hidden layers.
Neurons in the first hidden layer create multiple hyperplanes that partition the input space into several half-spaces. 
Neurons in the following hidden layers combine these half-spaces to form more complex regions. 
The intersections of these half-spaces produce convex regions, such as polygons in two dimensions or polytopes in higher dimensions. 
The output layer combines these convex regions through unions, enabling the formation of complex decision boundaries that may be non-convex or even disconnected. 
This capability to construct intricate piecewise-linear decision boundaries makes MLPs universal approximators, allowing them to approximate any continuous function when provided with enough hidden neurons and suitable activation functions.

--- slide 7 ---
Input Layer
Acts as the entry point of the network. 
Simply receives the input features and forwards them to the next layer without performing any computation.
 
Hidden Layer(s)
Process the input data and learn underlying patterns. 
Allow the network to solve problems that cannot be separated by a single linear boundary. 
Even a single hidden layer can represent complex relationships, while additional hidden layers increase the network's ability to approximate highly complex functions with greater accuracy. 

Output Layer
The final processing layer of the network. 
Combines the learned information from the hidden layers and generates the predicted output.

Softmax Activation Functions
The Softmax function is commonly used in the output layer of neural networks for classification tasks. It transforms raw output scores (logits) into probability values by normalizing them, ensuring that all outputs lie between 0 and 1 and their sum equals 1.

Converts logits into probabilities. 

Output values range from 0 to 1 and sum to 1. 

Used in the final layer of classification networks.

Suitable for multi-class classification, where exactly one class is chosen as the prediction.
