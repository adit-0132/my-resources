Using a dense retriever: ColBERT
A question q is tokenized as [q1,...,qN], prepended with a
[CLS] and a special [Q] token, and passed through BERT to
get output vectors q = [q1,...,qN]. The passage d with tokens
[d1,...,dm], is processed similarly, including a [CLS] and
special [D] token.
A linear layer is applied on top of d and q to control the
output dimension, so as to keep the vectors small for storage
efficiency, and vectors are rescaled to unit length, producing
the final vector sequences Eq (length N) and Ed (length m).

Evaluation Metrics
Evaluation of Delexicalized Response Generation Task:
● Delexicalized-BLEU: BLEU score on responses with slot values replaced by slot
tags (e.g. [HOTEL_AREA])
● Inform: Measures how often all the entities provided by the system are correct
● Success: Reflects how often the system is able to correctly answer all the
requested attributes
● Entity-F1: F1-score for the entities returned to the user through the generated
response
Combined Score: Used for selecting best checkpoint/hyperparameters
or a model.
Score = BLEU + 0.5 (Inform + Success)

ROUGE-2 has been shown to correlate somewhat well with human judgments for multidocument extractive summarization tasks
● Re fe re nce summary (RS): The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
● Ge ne rate d summary (GS): A quick brown fox leaped over a lazy dog.
● Bigrams in RS: [the quick, quick brown, brown fox, fox jumps, jumps ove r, ove r the , the lazy, lazy
dog]
● Bigrams in GS: [a quick, quick brown, brown fox, fox le ape d, le ape d ove r, ove r a, a lazy, lazy dog]
● Ove rlapping Bigrams: [quick brown, brown fox, lazy dog] (3 ove rlapping bigrams)
● ROUGE-2 = Ove rlapping Bigrams / Bigrams in RS = 3 / 8

we can use the weighted log probability of one text y

Precision (r → h): from reference text to
system-generated text p(h|r,θ). This direction
assesses how likely the hypothesis could be
constructed based on the gold reference
Faithfulness (s → h): from source document to
hypothesis p(h|s,θ). This direction measures
how likely it is that the hypothesis could be
generated based on the source text.
Recall (h → r): from system-generated text to
reference text p(r|h,θ). This version quantifies
how easily a gold reference could be generated
by the hypothesis
