Deep neural networks
• Networks with more than one hidden layer
• With ReLU activation, both shallow and deep neural networks
describe piecewise linear mappings from input to output
• As the number of hidden units increases, the shallow NNs improve
their descriptive power.
• With enough hidden units, shallow networks can describe arbitrarily
complex functions in high dimensions.
• Then why go for Deep neural networks?
*Slides adapted from http://udlbook.com
--- page 60 ---
Why Deep Neural Networks?
Deep networks can produce many more linear regions than
shallow networks for the same number of parameters!
Montufar, Guido F., et al. "On the number of linear regions of deep neural networks." Advances in neural information processing systems 27 (2014).
